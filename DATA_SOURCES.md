# Repack Pilot: data sources

Repack Pilot uses no data from a user or a client and no personal data. Everything on the page is synthetic, a sourced input of the engine, or an assumption, and says which.

> **Fictional retailer.** Ruhrkorb, its DC in Unna, its stores, orders, items and robot cell are invented. The towns are real places in North Rhine-Westphalia; no real store, company or person in them is described, and no robot vendor is named.

## Engine inputs

| Symbol | Input | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| `N` | Units repacked a year (Your DC) | 6,000,000 | Assumption: a regional DC, about 24,000 units a day on 250 days |
| `r_m` | Picks an hour by hand (Your DC) | 400 | IML |
| `c` | Labour cost an hour (Your DC) | €35.80 | EUROSTAT_LC |
| `s` | Share of units the robot may take | 70 % | Assumption, proxy KNAPP |
| `p` | Grasp success | 91 % | Assumption, proxy REGISTER |
| `r_c` | Grasp attempts an hour | 500 | Assumption: the low end of IML's range |
| | Nights a year, shift length | 250, 8 h | The build brief |

The formulas are in `js/engine.js`. Labour per 1,000 units by hand is 1,000 ÷ `r_m` hours; with the cell it falls by the share `s × p`; a year saves `N ÷ r_m × s × p` hours at `c`, gross, before cell costs; a cell pays while its all-in cost an operating hour stays below `r_c × p ÷ r_m × c`.

## The night

| Value | Basis |
|---|---|
| 3,000 units for 12 stores, truck times from 01:35 (Essen) to 06:00 (Schwerte) | Synthetic, calibrated so every store makes its truck with the cell and several miss it by hand |
| 24 items: 18 the robot may take (2,100 units), 6 for people (900 units): eggs, a six-pack of water over the cell's 3 kg payload, potatoes, bananas, red wine and basil | Synthetic; the per-item grasp rates average exactly 91 % over tonight's units |
| Apricot jam in a glass jar: 200 g in the item master, the jam's net weight; the scale reads 400 g | Synthetic on purpose, after the cup example in WP_DETECON |
| Barcode 4037848020859 on 6 units at 23:10, unknown to the item master | Synthetic arrival of a real product number |
| A reading more than 25 % off the model's weight is written back for every unit of the item | Assumption |
| Unknown barcode, a fragile item gripped with low confidence, or grasp success below 75 % over 100 attempts: the unit or the cell goes to a person | Assumption, rules in `js/escalation.js` |
| Grasp attempt 7.2 s, manual pick 9 s, totes up to 15 kg | From `r_c` and `r_m`; tote limit an assumption |
| Seed 7 | The night is the same in every browser |

`data/world.js` is generated by `tools/build_world.mjs`, which also documents how the units are spread over the stores.
